I grew up on Lake Iseo. The yellow walkway is for Christo and Jeanne-Claude’s The Floating Piers, which crossed the lake to Monte Isola in the summer of 2016. The water beside it runs on the wave equation, the kind of physics a world model has to get right: move across it, or tap it, and the walkway rides the ripples.
