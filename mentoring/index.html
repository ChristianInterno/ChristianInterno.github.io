I grew up on Lake Iseo. The walkway is for Christo and Jeanne-Claude’s The Floating Piers, which crossed the lake to Monte Isola in the summer of 2016. The water beside it follows linear wave theory for deep water, ω² = gk + (σ/ρ)k³: the breeze’s long waves roll in slowly, and the short ripples from a touch run out ahead of them. Move across the water, or tap it.
